Why digital‑first wins

Determinism → truth source. You get exact, replayable outputs (frames + logs) that the analog path must match.

Faster iteration. Features, bugs, and perf work are 10× easier before cameras, DACs, jitter, and noise enter the chat.

Same IR both ways. If we freeze the IR/tiles now, the analog backend just becomes “another device driver.”
