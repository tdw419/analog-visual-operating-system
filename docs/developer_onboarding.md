# The PXOS Developer Onboarding Funnel

This document outlines the structured journey for developers engaging with the PXOS ecosystem, from initial discovery to becoming a core contributor or launching a production application. Our goal is to create a frictionless, supportive, and rewarding experience.

## Stage 1: Awareness & Discovery (Top of Funnel)

This stage is about attracting developers and making them aware of what PXOS is and why it's a compelling platform to build on.

-   **Channels**:
    -   High-quality content (blog posts, articles) on platforms like Medium, Dev.to, and HackerNoon.
    -   Active presence on developer-centric social media (X, Farcaster).
    -   Participation in major blockchain and enterprise tech conferences.
    -   Targeted outreach to university blockchain clubs and enterprise R&D departments.
-   **Messaging**:
    -   Focus on the unique value proposition: solving the scalability/security/interoperability trilemma for enterprise use cases.
    -   Highlight the hybrid architecture as a key differentiator.
-   **Key Metric**: Growth in website traffic, social media followers, and newsletter subscribers.

## Stage 2: Education & Exploration (Middle of Funnel)

Once developers are aware of PXOS, this stage is about providing them with the resources they need to learn and experiment.

-   **Resources**:
    -   **Comprehensive Documentation**: Clear, well-structured docs with conceptual explanations, API references, and tutorials.
    -   **Interactive Tutorials**: A browser-based, "Hello World" style tutorial that lets developers write and deploy their first simple dApp in minutes.
    -   **Developer Quickstart Guide**: A single, clear guide to setting up a local development environment, getting testnet tokens, and running a node.
    -   **Video Content**: A series of short, engaging video tutorials on the official PXOS YouTube channel.
-   **Key Metric**: Number of developers who complete the quickstart guide and deploy a contract to the testnet.

## Stage 3: Activation & Building (Bottom of Funnel)

This stage is about converting educated developers into active builders on the platform.

-   **Initiatives**:
    -   **PXOS Grant Program**: Provide funding for promising projects (as detailed in `grant_program_examples.md`).
    -   **Hackathons**: Regular online and in-person hackathons with attractive prizes and mentorship from the core team.
    -   **Technical Support**: A dedicated Discord channel (`#developer-support`) and regular office hours with core engineers.
    -   **Boilerplate Repositories**: A collection of open-source boilerplate projects (e.g., for a DEX, an NFT marketplace, a DID service) that developers can fork and build upon.
-   **Key Metric**: Number of grant applications, hackathon participants, and active projects on the testnet.

## Stage 4: Retention & Advocacy (Post-Funnel)

Once developers are building on PXOS, this stage is about retaining them and turning them into advocates for the platform.

-   **Programs**:
    -   **Ambassador Program**: Recognize and reward active community members who contribute to the ecosystem through content creation, event organization, and peer support.
    -   **Contributor Pathway**: A clear, documented path for community developers to contribute to the core PXOS protocol, with the potential to become a core developer.
    -   **dApp Showcase**: A section on the official PXOS website to feature and promote high-quality applications built on the network.
-   **Key Metric**: Developer retention rate, number of community-led initiatives, and number of core protocol contributions from the community.
