# PXOS Tokenomics Model v1.0

## 1. Introduction

The PXOS token is the native utility token of the PXOS network. It is designed to be an integral part of the ecosystem, providing economic incentives that secure the network, empower the community, and drive long-term, sustainable growth. The tokenomics model is structured to align the interests of all network participants, including delegates, dApp developers, and end-users.

## 2. Token Utility

The PXOS token has three primary functions:

-   **Staking & Governance**: Token holders can stake their PXOS to participate in the Delegated Proof-of-Stake (DPoS) consensus mechanism. Staked tokens grant voting power, allowing holders to elect the delegates who produce blocks and govern the network. Stakers receive a portion of the network rewards.
-   **Transaction Fees**: The PXOS token is used to pay for transaction fees on the network. This includes simple transfers, smart contract executions, and fees for using dApps. A portion of these fees will be burned, creating a deflationary pressure on the token supply.
-   **Ecosystem Incentives**: The PXOS token is used to fund the ecosystem grant program, rewarding developers and teams who build valuable applications and tools on the network.

## 3. Token Distribution

-   **Total Supply**: 1,000,000,000 PXOS (fixed).
-   **Ecosystem Fund**: 30% - Vested over 5 years. To be used for grants, community initiatives, and marketing.
-   **Team & Advisors**: 20% - Vested over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
-   **Staking Rewards**: 20% - To be distributed to stakers and delegates over the first 5 years of the network.
-   **Private Sale**: 15% - Strategic partners and early backers.
-   **Public Sale**: 10% - To be distributed to the public.
-   **Foundation Treasury**: 5% - For long-term operational costs and strategic partnerships.

## 4. Economic Model

### 4.1 Staking Rewards

-   **Inflationary Model**: The staking rewards will be sourced from a pre-allocated pool, representing a controlled, decreasing inflation rate over the first 5 years.
-   **Reward Distribution**: Rewards will be distributed proportionally to the amount of PXOS staked. Delegates will receive a commission on the rewards generated by their stakers.

### 4.2 Fee Mechanism

-   **Gas Model**: Transactions will consume "gas," with the price determined by network congestion. Fees are paid in PXOS.
-   **Fee Burning**: A portion of every transaction fee (e.g., 25%) will be permanently burned. This introduces a deflationary mechanism that will increase in effect as network usage grows, rewarding long-term holders. The remaining fees will be distributed to the block-producing delegates.

### 4.3 Long-Term Sustainability

The combination of a fixed total supply, a deflationary fee-burning mechanism, and a robust ecosystem funded by grants is designed to create a sustainable economic model. As the network matures and the initial staking rewards diminish, the transaction fees will become the primary source of income for delegates, ensuring the long-term security and viability of the network.
