# PXOS Regulatory Strategy Overview v1.0

## 1. Our Proactive Stance

The regulatory landscape for blockchain and digital assets is rapidly evolving and varies significantly across jurisdictions. The PXOS project is committed to a proactive, good-faith approach to regulatory compliance. Our strategy is not to avoid regulation, but to engage with it constructively, ensuring the long-term sustainability and legitimacy of the PXOS network.

## 2. Core Principles

-   **Compliance-by-Design**: We are building the PXOS platform with regulatory considerations in mind. The hybrid architecture, which separates public and private data, is a key example of this, allowing enterprises to meet their specific data privacy and compliance obligations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
-   **Transparency**: We will be transparent about the design of the protocol, the function of the PXOS token, and the structure of our governance model.
-   **Cooperation**: We will seek to cooperate with regulators and policymakers to help shape a clear and fair regulatory framework for decentralized technologies.

## 3. Key Jurisdictions & Focus Areas

Our initial focus will be on ensuring compliance in key jurisdictions with developing or established regulatory frameworks for digital assets, such as:

-   Switzerland (FINMA)
-   Singapore (MAS)
-   The European Union (MiCA)
-   United Arab Emirates (VARA)

Our legal teams will continuously monitor the regulatory landscape in these and other regions to adapt our strategy as needed.

## 4. The PXOS Token: A Utility Token

The PXOS token is designed as a **utility token**, and its primary purpose is to enable participation in and use of the PXOS network. Its functions (staking for security, paying for transaction fees, and incentivizing ecosystem development) are integral to the operation of the network. We will take care in all our communications to ensure that the token is presented as a utility token and not as a speculative investment or security.

## 5. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Know Your Customer (KYC)

-   **Protocol Level**: The PXOS protocol itself is neutral. It does not inherently require AML/KYC for peer-to-peer transactions on the public network.
-   **Application Level**: We will provide tools and guidance for dApp developers and enterprises building on PXOS to implement their own AML/KYC procedures as required by the regulations in their respective jurisdictions. For example, a security token platform built on PXOS would be responsible for implementing KYC for its users.
-   **Token Sales**: For any official public sale of the PXOS token, we will partner with a regulated entity to conduct full AML/KYC checks on all participants.

## 6. Ongoing Engagement

This document represents a high-level overview. Our regulatory strategy will be a living process, involving ongoing legal counsel, engagement with industry bodies (like the Crypto Valley Association or the Blockchain Association), and direct dialogue with regulators where possible.
